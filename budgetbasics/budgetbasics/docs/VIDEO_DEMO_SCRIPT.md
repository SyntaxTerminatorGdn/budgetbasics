# BudgetBasics MP4 Demonstration Script

The SRS requires a recorded MP4 showing the working website. This file is the shot list and narration outline; it is not a video recording.

## Suggested 5-minute walkthrough

1. **Welcome and header (0:00–0:30):** Show the brand/tagline, live date and clock, local visitor counter label, currency selector, tip ticker, dark-mode control, and full module navigation. Mention that the displayed visit count is local to the browser.
2. **Learning basics (0:30–1:00):** Show the income/fixed/variable concepts, select the Dorm Resident sample, expand its itemized budget, and answer a knowledge-check question.
3. **Needs, wants, and mistakes (1:00–1:40):** Classify a sample item, show feedback and the purchase-delay guide, and expand two Money Mistakes scenarios.
4. **Budget and savings tools (1:40–2:40):** Calculate a `$600` monthly budget, show `$300 / $180 / $120`, and read the educational note. Enter a Study Laptop goal (`$600`, `$150` saved, `$75` monthly) to show the six-month estimate.
5. **Expense planner (2:40–3:20):** Add an expense, edit it, remove it, show total and remaining sample balance, then export a CSV using the visible button.
6. **Search and gallery (3:20–4:10):** Search for `saving`, filter and sort resources, filter the gallery by Budgeting, review infographic captions and alternative text, and show the printable checklist.
7. **Chat, feedback, and contact (4:10–5:00):** Ask a suggested chatbot question, show the disclaimer, open the feedback and contact sections, then show the sitemap and back-to-top control.

## Recording checklist

- Record the real application in a desktop browser with the address bar or application context visible.
- Capture readable controls and keep the pointer out of headings and values.
- Demonstrate at least one valid calculation and one validation message.
- Show the user-initiated CSV export and printable checklist without exposing personal information.
- Export as `.mp4` and include the recording in the final project ZIP.
